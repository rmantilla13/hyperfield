Hyperfield

An open studio for video, stills and 3D on your own GPU. The brand is a deep field: the interface stays dark and quiet, and the work gives off the light.

01

The mark

A field of stars bent around one, with that star's orbit. The eight points pull away from the centre the way light bends around a mass. The star in the middle is the work, and the rest of the field is in orbit around it. Below 20px the field drops away and only the star, its ring and the planet remain.

02

Color

Cool hull metal in deep space. Nebula violet is the one signal color. Generate is plasma, a violet-to-ion gradient. Text is starlight, never pure white. Every tint in the stylesheet comes from a channel token such as --accent-rgb, so a palette change there is a single edit. The logo, favicon, 3D accents and the phone and judge pages carry the hex values directly.

05

Rules

  • The work is the brightest thing on screen. The nebula glow and the star dust stay faint, and the star dust never moves.
  • One signal color. Nebula violet marks what is selected or about to happen. Ion cyan is only for secondary information and the far end of the plasma.
  • Starlight, not white. Use #eceffc for text and starlight channels for hairlines. Pure white is only for highlights on the plasma.
  • The mark lives on dark. Don't recolor the ring, rotate the orbit, or put the mark on a light or busy background without its hull square.
